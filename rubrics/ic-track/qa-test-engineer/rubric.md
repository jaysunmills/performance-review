# QA/Test Engineer Competency Rubric

This rubric defines the role-specific competencies for QA/Test Engineers at each level. All engineers are also evaluated on the three [Universal Criteria](../../../docs/universal-criteria.md): Craft/Quality, Speed, and Adaptiveness.

---

## Level Overview

| Level | Title | Experience | Key Competencies |
|-------|-------|------------|------------------|
| IC1 | Junior QA Engineer | 0-2 years | Testing Quality, Learning Agility |
| IC2 | QA Engineer | 2-4 years | Testing Quality, Test Automation, Collaboration |
| IC3 | Senior QA Engineer | 4-7 years | Testing Quality, Test Architecture, Quality Strategy, Mentoring |
| IC4 | Staff QA Engineer | 7-12 years | Technical Leadership, Quality Architecture, Cross-Team Collaboration, Strategic Thinking |

---

## IC1: Junior QA Engineer

### Scope
Task execution within defined boundaries. Focus on learning testing fundamentals.

### Competencies

#### Testing Quality

**Definition**: Executing tests and documenting results clearly

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Executes test cases thoroughly
- Documents bugs clearly and completely
- Identifies edge cases and scenarios
- Follows testing standards and practices
- Provides useful reproduction steps

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Tests work but miss scenarios
- Bug reports lack detail
- Requires guidance on standards

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Tests are incomplete
- Bug reports unclear
- Cannot follow testing processes

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study testing fundamentals
- Learn bug reporting best practices
- Practice exploratory testing
- Review quality metrics

#### Learning Agility

**Definition**: Quickly learning testing tools and domain knowledge

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Asks thoughtful questions about product
- Learns testing tools proactively
- Understands product functionality
- Applies feedback from reviews
- Shows steady improvement

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Slow to learn new tools
- Limited product understanding
- Doesn't apply feedback

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Cannot learn testing concepts
- Makes same mistakes repeatedly

## IC2: QA Engineer

### Scope
Test ownership with independence. Expected to own test suites and automation.

### Competencies

#### Testing Quality

**Definition**: Creating comprehensive test coverage for features

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Designs thorough test strategies
- Creates comprehensive test cases
- Identifies boundary conditions and edge cases
- Validates requirements and acceptance criteria
- Considers security and performance testing

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Good coverage but misses edge cases
- Limited test strategy
- Reactive testing only

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Poor test coverage
- Misses obvious bugs
- Doesn't improve from feedback

#### Test Automation

**Definition**: Building and maintaining automated test suites

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Writes clean, maintainable automated tests
- Creates reliable, non-flaky tests
- Chooses appropriate automation levels
- Integrates tests with CI/CD
- Maintains automation framework

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Tests work but are flaky
- Poor test organization
- Doesn't maintain tests

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Automation creates more problems
- Cannot write automated tests
- Ignores failing tests

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study test automation patterns
- Learn testing frameworks deeply
- Practice writing stable tests
- Review automation best practices

#### Collaboration

**Definition**: Working effectively with development teams on quality

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Partners with developers early in features
- Communicates quality risks clearly
- Provides constructive feedback
- Helps developers write testable code
- Shares testing knowledge with team

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited developer engagement
- Waits until end to test
- Adversarial relationship

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Creates friction with developers
- Blocks releases without alternatives
- "Quality police" mentality

## IC3: Senior QA Engineer

### Scope
Technical leadership within the QA team. Expected to lead quality initiatives.

### Competencies

#### Testing Quality

**Definition**: Setting quality standards and practices for the team

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Test strategies serve as examples
- Establishes quality best practices
- Reviews test plans and automation
- Creates reusable testing patterns
- Champions quality culture

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Good personal quality but doesn't elevate team
- Doesn't create standards

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Quality regression
- Negative impact on quality culture

#### Test Architecture

**Definition**: Designing test infrastructure and frameworks

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Designs test architecture for scalability
- Creates test infrastructure
- Balances different test types (unit, integration, e2e)
- Optimizes test execution time
- Plans test data management

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Good designs but poor adoption
- Doesn't consider maintenance
- Over-engineers solutions

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Architecture causes problems
- Cannot design at this complexity

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study test architecture patterns
- Learn about test infrastructure
- Practice designing test strategies
- Review test metrics and optimization

#### Quality Strategy

**Definition**: Developing quality strategies for products and teams

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Defines quality strategy for products
- Sets meaningful quality metrics
- Balances manual and automated testing
- Plans for different testing types
- Adjusts strategy based on results

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Tactical quality work only
- Doesn't define metrics
- Cannot develop strategy

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No quality strategy
- Cannot think strategically

#### Mentoring

**Definition**: Developing junior QA engineers

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Actively mentors 1-2 QA engineers
- Shares testing knowledge and intuition
- Reviews work and provides feedback
- Helps mentees develop quality judgment
- Creates learning opportunities

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Answers questions but doesn't mentor
- Hoards knowledge

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Avoids mentoring
- Negative impact on mentees

---

## IC4: Staff QA Engineer

### Scope
Cross-team quality impact. Expected to drive quality strategy across organization.

### Competencies

#### Technical Leadership

**Definition**: Leading quality direction across organization

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Sets quality direction adopted org-wide
- Drives consensus on quality decisions
- Unblocks teams on quality challenges
- Builds credibility as quality expert
- Balances quality with velocity

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Leads within team but not across org
- Direction not adopted

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Unable to lead at this scope
- Creates quality silos

#### Quality Architecture

**Definition**: Designing organization-wide quality architecture

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Designs quality architecture for org
- Creates test infrastructure and tooling
- Enables self-service quality capabilities
- Plans multi-year quality roadmap
- Evaluates tools and vendors

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Good designs but limited adoption
- Doesn't consider org constraints

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Architecture causes problems
- Cannot operate at this scope

#### Cross-Team Collaboration

**Definition**: Working across teams on quality initiatives

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Builds relationships across org
- Facilitates cross-team quality discussions
- Shares quality capabilities
- Enables other teams with quality tools
- Gathers feedback on quality experience

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited to familiar teams
- Doesn't share capabilities

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Creates friction
- Siloed thinking

#### Strategic Thinking

**Definition**: Connecting quality work to business objectives

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Understands business quality needs
- Proposes quality initiatives aligned with business
- Thinks 1-2 years ahead on quality
- Makes trade-offs with business context
- Quantifies quality ROI

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Quality focus without business connection
- Short-term thinking

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Decisions misaligned with business
