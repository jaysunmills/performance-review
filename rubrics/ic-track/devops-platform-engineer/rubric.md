# DevOps/Platform Engineer Competency Rubric

This rubric defines the role-specific competencies for DevOps/Platform Engineers at each level. All engineers are also evaluated on the three [Universal Criteria](../../../docs/universal-criteria.md): Craft/Quality, Speed, and Adaptiveness.

---

## Level Overview

| Level | Title | Experience | Key Competencies |
|-------|-------|------------|------------------|
| IC1 | Junior DevOps Engineer | 0-2 years | Infrastructure Quality, Learning Agility |
| IC2 | DevOps Engineer | 2-4 years | Infrastructure Quality, Reliability Engineering, Collaboration |
| IC3 | Senior DevOps Engineer | 4-7 years | Infrastructure Quality, Platform Design, Reliability Engineering, Mentoring |
| IC4 | Staff Platform Engineer | 7-12 years | Technical Leadership, Platform Architecture, Cross-Team Collaboration, Strategic Thinking |
| IC5 | Principal Platform Engineer | 12+ years | Technical Vision, Innovation, Organizational Impact, Industry Expertise |

---

## IC1: Junior DevOps Engineer

### Scope
Task execution within defined boundaries. Focus on learning infrastructure fundamentals.

### Competencies

#### Infrastructure Quality

**Definition**: Writing clean, tested infrastructure-as-code

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Writes clean, readable IaC following team standards
- Includes appropriate tests and validation
- Documents infrastructure components
- Uses version control properly
- Follows security best practices

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- IaC works but has style/readability issues
- Missing tests or documentation
- Requires multiple review rounds

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- IaC frequently broken
- Ignores security practices
- No documentation

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study team IaC patterns (Terraform, etc.)
- Learn cloud provider fundamentals
- Practice writing testable infrastructure
- Review production runbooks

#### Learning Agility

**Definition**: Quickly learning infrastructure tools and concepts

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Asks thoughtful questions about systems
- Learns cloud services and tools proactively
- Understands networking and security basics
- Applies feedback from reviews
- Shows steady improvement

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Slow to learn new tools
- Doesn't understand system context
- Limited initiative

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Cannot apply basic concepts
- Makes same mistakes repeatedly

## IC2: DevOps Engineer

### Scope
System ownership with independence. Expected to own infrastructure components.

### Competencies

#### Infrastructure Quality

**Definition**: Building production-ready infrastructure with proper controls

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Delivers secure, scalable infrastructure
- Implements proper monitoring and alerting
- Considers cost optimization
- Creates clear runbooks and documentation
- Automates repetitive tasks

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Infrastructure works but lacks robustness
- Limited monitoring
- Manual processes remain

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Frequent infrastructure failures
- Security vulnerabilities
- Poor documentation

#### Reliability Engineering

**Definition**: Building and maintaining reliable systems

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Implements effective monitoring and alerting
- Responds to and resolves incidents effectively
- Conducts blameless postmortems
- Improves reliability based on learnings
- Manages on-call responsibilities well

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Reactive to incidents, not proactive
- Doesn't learn from incidents
- Limited monitoring coverage

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Cannot resolve incidents
- Creates reliability problems

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study SRE practices
- Learn monitoring and observability
- Practice incident response
- Review postmortems

#### Collaboration

**Definition**: Working effectively with development teams

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Partners with developers on deployments
- Communicates changes and impacts clearly
- Helps teams improve their practices
- Provides good developer experience
- Balances security with usability

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited communication
- Gatekeeper mentality
- Doesn't understand dev needs

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Creates friction with developers
- Blocks progress without alternatives

## IC3: Senior DevOps Engineer

### Scope
Technical leadership within the platform team. Expected to lead platform projects.

### Competencies

#### Infrastructure Quality

**Definition**: Setting infrastructure standards and practices for the team

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Infrastructure serves as example for team
- Establishes IaC best practices
- Identifies and addresses infrastructure debt
- Reviews for quality and security
- Champions automation culture

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Good personal quality but doesn't elevate team
- Doesn't address technical debt

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Quality regression
- Negative impact on standards

#### Platform Design

**Definition**: Designing platform solutions for developer needs

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Designs self-service platform capabilities
- Creates intuitive developer interfaces
- Balances flexibility with guardrails
- Documents platform features clearly
- Considers adoption and usability

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Good designs but poor adoption
- Doesn't consider user experience

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Platform causes problems
- Cannot design at this complexity

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study internal developer platforms
- Learn about developer experience
- Practice platform thinking
- Get feedback from developers

#### Reliability Engineering

**Definition**: Leading reliability practices for the organization

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Establishes SLOs and error budgets
- Leads incident response improvements
- Creates reliability standards
- Teaches reliability practices to others
- Balances reliability with velocity

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Good practices but doesn't spread them
- Doesn't establish standards

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Cannot lead reliability at this level

#### Mentoring

**Definition**: Developing junior platform engineers

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Actively mentors 1-2 junior engineers
- Shares infrastructure knowledge
- Reviews work and provides feedback
- Helps mentees develop on-call skills
- Creates learning opportunities

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Answers questions but doesn't mentor
- Doesn't share knowledge

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Avoids mentoring
- Negative impact on mentees

---

## IC4: Staff Platform Engineer

### Scope
Cross-team platform impact. Expected to drive platform strategy.

### Competencies

#### Technical Leadership

**Definition**: Leading platform direction across organization

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Sets platform direction adopted org-wide
- Drives consensus on infrastructure decisions
- Unblocks teams on complex challenges
- Builds credibility as platform expert
- Balances standardization with flexibility

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Leads within team but not across org
- Direction not adopted

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Unable to lead at this scope
- Creates silos

#### Platform Architecture

**Definition**: Designing organization-wide platform architecture

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Designs platform architecture for org
- Creates golden paths for common needs
- Ensures security and compliance at scale
- Plans multi-year platform evolution
- Evaluates tools and vendors

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Good designs but limited adoption
- Doesn't consider org constraints

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Architecture causes problems
- Cannot operate at this scope

#### Cross-Team Collaboration

**Definition**: Working across teams on platform initiatives

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Builds relationships across org
- Facilitates cross-team discussions
- Shares platform capabilities
- Enables other teams to succeed
- Gathers feedback systematically

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited to familiar teams
- Doesn't gather feedback

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Creates friction
- Siloed thinking

#### Strategic Thinking

**Definition**: Connecting platform work to business objectives

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Understands business needs for platform
- Proposes initiatives aligned with business
- Thinks 1-2 years ahead
- Makes trade-offs with business context
- Identifies cost optimization opportunities

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Technical focus without business connection
- Short-term thinking

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Decisions misaligned with business

---

## IC5: Principal Platform Engineer

### Scope
Organization-wide platform strategy and influence.

### Competencies

#### Technical Vision

**Definition**: Defining long-term platform direction

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Creates compelling platform vision
- Articulates multi-year roadmap
- Aligns with company strategy
- Inspires others with vision

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Has vision but cannot drive adoption

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No clear vision

#### Innovation

**Definition**: Driving platform innovation

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Evaluates emerging technologies
- Leads proof-of-concepts
- Drives valuable innovations
- Creates innovation culture

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Innovates but doesn't spread

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No innovation

#### Organizational Impact

**Definition**: Improving platform capabilities org-wide

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Improves engineering productivity org-wide
- Creates platform capabilities for company
- Raises platform bar for organization
- Develops other platform leaders

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Impact limited
- Doesn't develop others

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No organizational impact

#### Industry Expertise

**Definition**: Being a recognized platform expert externally

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Recognized platform expert
- Speaks at conferences
- Publishes content
- Brings external perspectives

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Expert internally only

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Not recognized as expert
