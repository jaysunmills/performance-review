# Technical Product Manager Competency Rubric

This rubric defines the role-specific competencies for Technical Product Managers at each level. All PMs are also evaluated on the three [Universal Criteria](../../../docs/universal-criteria.md): Craft/Quality, Speed, and Adaptiveness.

---

## Level Overview

| Level | Title | Experience | Key Competencies |
|-------|-------|------------|------------------|
| PM1 | Associate Product Manager | 0-2 years | Product Execution, User Understanding, Learning Agility |
| PM2 | Product Manager | 2-5 years | Product Strategy, User Research, Stakeholder Management, Technical Understanding |
| PM3 | Senior Product Manager | 5-8 years | Product Leadership, Market Strategy, Cross-Functional Leadership, Mentoring |
| PM4 | Staff Product Manager | 8-12 years | Portfolio Strategy, Business Strategy, Organizational Influence, Executive Communication |
| PM5 | Principal Product Manager | 12+ years | Product Vision, Innovation, Organizational Impact, Industry Expertise |

---

## PM1: Associate Product Manager

### Scope
Feature-level product work. Focus on learning PM fundamentals.

### Competencies

#### Product Execution

**Definition**: Defining and delivering product features

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Writes clear product requirements
- Defines acceptance criteria
- Prioritizes feature scope effectively
- Works with engineering to deliver
- Validates features meet user needs

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Requirements unclear
- Scope not well managed
- Poor engineering partnership

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Cannot define features
- Features don't meet needs

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study product frameworks
- Practice writing PRDs
- Learn user research methods
- Shadow senior PMs

#### User Understanding

**Definition**: Understanding user needs and problems

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Talks to users regularly
- Identifies user pain points
- Understands user workflows
- Validates assumptions with users
- Translates needs to requirements

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited user contact
- Assumes rather than validates

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No user understanding
- Builds wrong things

#### Learning Agility

**Definition**: Quickly learning product skills and domain

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Learns product tools and methods
- Understands business domain
- Applies feedback to improve
- Shows steady improvement

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Slow to learn PM skills
- Limited domain knowledge

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Cannot learn PM fundamentals

## PM2: Product Manager

### Scope
Product area ownership. Expected to own product roadmap.

### Competencies

#### Product Strategy

**Definition**: Developing product area strategy and roadmap

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Owns product area roadmap
- Prioritizes based on impact
- Defines success metrics
- Makes product trade-offs
- Aligns with business goals

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Roadmap unclear or reactive
- Poor prioritization
- Metrics not defined

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No product strategy
- Products don't deliver value

#### User Research

**Definition**: Conducting research to understand user needs

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Designs and conducts user research
- Analyzes research findings
- Synthesizes insights for team
- Validates hypotheses with data
- Uses multiple research methods

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited research skills
- Doesn't analyze effectively

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No user research
- Builds on assumptions

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study user research methods
- Practice interviewing users
- Learn data analysis
- Review research best practices

#### Stakeholder Management

**Definition**: Managing stakeholders across the organization

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Identifies all stakeholders
- Manages expectations effectively
- Communicates product plans clearly
- Builds relationships across teams
- Influences without authority

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited stakeholder engagement
- Poor communication
- Cannot influence

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Stakeholder relationships damaged

#### Technical Understanding

**Definition**: Understanding technical context for product decisions

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Understands technical constraints
- Evaluates technical trade-offs
- Partners effectively with engineering
- Translates technical to user terms
- Knows system capabilities

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited technical understanding
- Poor engineering partnership

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Technical gaps cause issues

## PM3: Senior Product Manager

### Scope
Product line leadership. Expected to drive product vision.

### Competencies

#### Product Leadership

**Definition**: Leading product line strategy

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Leads product line strategy
- Drives compelling product vision
- Makes tough product decisions
- Handles ambiguity well
- Delivers significant outcomes

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited strategic scope
- Cannot make tough decisions

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Product line underperforms
- Cannot lead at this level

#### Market Strategy

**Definition**: Understanding market and competitive landscape

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Understands competitive landscape
- Identifies market opportunities
- Positions products effectively
- Anticipates market changes
- Develops go-to-market strategies

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited market knowledge
- Doesn't anticipate changes

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Products miss market

**Development Suggestions:**
- Study market analysis
- Track competitors regularly
- Learn go-to-market
- Build market relationships

#### Cross-Functional Leadership

**Definition**: Leading across organizational boundaries

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Leads cross-functional teams
- Drives alignment across functions
- Resolves cross-team conflicts
- Influences without authority
- Enables collaboration

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited cross-functional influence
- Cannot resolve conflicts

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Creates cross-team friction

#### Mentoring

**Definition**: Developing junior product managers

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Actively mentors 1-2 PMs
- Shares product knowledge
- Provides feedback on work
- Helps develop PM judgment
- Creates learning opportunities

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Doesn't proactively mentor
- Limited knowledge sharing

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Avoids mentoring

## PM4: Staff Product Manager

### Scope
Product portfolio leadership. Expected to lead most critical products.

### Competencies

#### Portfolio Strategy

**Definition**: Managing product portfolio strategy

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Drives product portfolio strategy
- Prioritizes products strategically
- Balances portfolio investments
- Identifies portfolio opportunities
- Optimizes portfolio value

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited portfolio view
- Poor prioritization

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Portfolio mismanaged

#### Business Strategy

**Definition**: Connecting products to business strategy

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Understands business deeply
- Drives product revenue/growth
- Identifies business opportunities
- Partners with business leadership
- Creates business cases

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited business connection
- Products don't drive business

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Products misaligned with business

#### Organizational Influence

**Definition**: Influencing product practices org-wide

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Influences product practices org-wide
- Creates PM standards and frameworks
- Develops PM community
- Builds PM capabilities
- Represents PM function

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Limited organizational influence
- Doesn't improve practices

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No organizational influence

#### Executive Communication

**Definition**: Communicating effectively with executives

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Communicates to executives effectively
- Influences product direction
- Provides appropriate detail
- Builds executive trust
- Represents products well

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Poor executive communication
- Cannot influence executives

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Damages trust with executives

---

## PM5: Principal Product Manager

### Scope
Organization-wide product strategy and innovation.

### Competencies

#### Product Vision

**Definition**: Defining long-term product direction

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Creates compelling product vision
- Articulates multi-year roadmap
- Aligns with company strategy
- Inspires others with vision
- Evolves vision strategically

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Has vision but cannot drive adoption

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No clear product vision

#### Innovation

**Definition**: Driving product innovation

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Identifies new product opportunities
- Leads innovation initiatives
- Drives valuable new products
- Creates innovation culture
- Balances innovation with core

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Innovates but doesn't spread

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No innovation

#### Organizational Impact

**Definition**: Improving product capabilities org-wide

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Improves PM productivity org-wide
- Creates product frameworks for company
- Raises product bar for organization
- Develops other product leaders
- Influences hiring standards

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Impact limited
- Doesn't develop others

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- No organizational impact

#### Industry Expertise

**Definition**: Being recognized product expert externally

**Level 4 - Meets Expectations:**
- Recognized product expert
- Speaks at conferences
- Publishes product content
- Brings external perspectives
- Builds industry relationships

**Level 3 - Needs Improvement:**
- Expert internally only

**Level 2/1 - Below Expectations:**
- Not recognized as expert
