Performance Review Process Flow

Employee Input

Self-Assessment

Employee reflects on their own performance across all criteria and competencies. Provides evidence and examples.
⏱️ 30-45 minutes
Peer Input (3-5 people)

360 Feedback Surveys

Colleagues provide quick multiple-choice ratings and brief narrative feedback about collaboration and impact.
⏱️ 10-15 minutes each
Manager Input

Ratings & Notes

Manager sets 1-4 ratings for each criterion, selects competencies, and adds observations and context.
⏱️ 45-60 minutes
