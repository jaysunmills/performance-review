# Performance Scoring System

## Overview

This performance management system uses a 1-4 scale where **4 is the target for everyone**. Unlike traditional curve-based systems, there is no forced distribution—every team member can achieve a 4 rating by meeting clearly defined standards.

## Scoring Scale

### Score 4: Meets Expectations (Target)

**This is the goal for all team members.**

**Definition**: Completes work in reasonable time AND proactively unblocks self

**Characteristics**:
- Delivers quality work within expected timeframes
- Asks for help when blocked (doesn't wait to be rescued)
- Communicates proactively about progress and obstacles
- Takes ownership of outcomes
- Adapts to changing requirements and feedback

**Example Behaviors**:
- "I'm stuck on X. I've tried A and B. Can you help me think through other options?"
- Raises blockers in standups before they become critical
- Seeks feedback early and incorporates it
- Volunteers for stretch assignments

### Score 3: Needs Improvement

**Definition**: Does good work but doesn't speak up when blocked

**Characteristics**:
- Quality of work is acceptable
- Struggles with self-advocacy
- Waits too long to escalate issues
- May have a "didn't want to bother anyone" mentality
- Work often takes longer than necessary due to unaddressed blockers

**Example Behaviors**:
- "I was stuck for two days but didn't want to interrupt anyone"
- Misses deadlines due to unreported obstacles
- Delivers work but with unnecessary delays
- Reluctant to ask clarifying questions

**Coaching Focus**: Build psychological safety, encourage early escalation, practice asking for help

### Score 2: Below Expectations

**Definition**: Significant issues with speed, quality, or adaptiveness

**Characteristics**:
- Work quality frequently below standards
- Execution pace consistently slow
- Resistance to feedback or change
- Requires excessive oversight
- May have skill gaps that aren't being addressed

**Example Behaviors**:
- Repeated quality issues after feedback
- Consistent missed deadlines
- "That's not how we did it at my last company"
- Defensive response to code review comments

**Action Required**: Performance improvement plan, clear milestones, regular check-ins

### Score 1: Unacceptable

**Definition**: Critical performance issues requiring immediate intervention

**Characteristics**:
- Fundamental inability to perform role requirements
- Serious quality or behavioral issues
- May be negatively impacting team
- Unwilling or unable to improve

**Action Required**: Immediate intervention, potential role change or separation

## Compensation Eligibility

### Requirement: "All 4s"

To be eligible for:
- Merit increases
- Stock refreshers
- Promotions

Team members must achieve a score of 4 in:
- All three universal criteria (Craft/Quality, Speed, Adaptiveness)
- All role-specific competencies for their level

### Partial Achievement

- Any score of 3: Coaching plan, may receive partial compensation increase
- Any score of 2: Performance improvement plan, no compensation increase
- Any score of 1: Immediate action required, no compensation increase

## Evaluation Frequency

### Formal Reviews
- **Cadence**: Bi-annual (or as defined by organization)
- **Documentation**: Full rubric completion with evidence

### Check-ins
- **Cadence**: Monthly or quarterly
- **Purpose**: Progress tracking, early identification of issues

### Continuous Feedback
- **Cadence**: Ongoing
- **Purpose**: Real-time course correction, positive reinforcement

## Key Principles

### 1. No Forced Distribution

Unlike stack ranking or bell curve systems:
- There is no quota for 4s, 3s, 2s, or 1s
- A team can (and should!) have all 4s
- Ratings reflect individual performance, not relative ranking

### 2. Transparency

- All criteria are documented and shared
- Ratings should never be a surprise
- Evidence-based evaluation, not subjective opinion

### 3. Growth Mindset

- A 3 rating is an opportunity for development
- Focus on "how to get to 4" not "why you're not a 4"
- Support and resources provided for improvement

### 4. Consistency

- Same standards applied across the organization
- Calibration sessions to ensure alignment
- Regular review of criteria for relevance

## Manager Responsibilities

1. **Set Clear Expectations**: Ensure direct reports understand criteria
2. **Provide Regular Feedback**: Don't wait for formal reviews
3. **Document Evidence**: Keep records of performance observations
4. **Support Development**: Create plans for improvement
5. **Calibrate**: Participate in calibration to ensure consistency

## Employee Responsibilities

1. **Know Your Criteria**: Review role-specific rubric
2. **Self-Assess Honestly**: Identify your own gaps
3. **Seek Feedback**: Don't wait to be told
4. **Ask for Help**: Proactively unblock yourself
5. **Document Accomplishments**: Track your own evidence
